# 2023 generation mix finding

The source's annual averages place nuclear first at 53.27%, hydro second at 25.13%, and gas third
at 12.65% of grid-connected output. Wind averages 8.21%; solar and biofuel are below 1% each in
this table. Percentages are rounded source values and apply only to the included measurement scope.
