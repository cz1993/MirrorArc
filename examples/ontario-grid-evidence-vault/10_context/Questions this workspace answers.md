# Questions this workspace answers

- How did annual Ontario electricity demand change from 2022 to 2023?
- Which month recorded the widest peak-to-minimum demand range in the included 2023 series?
- What generation sources dominated the 2023 grid-connected mix?
- How did imports and exports differ by quarter and neighbouring region?
- Which evidence may be redistributed, and which sources are metadata-only?
- Why is the illustrative forecast publication state suppressed?

Each answer should cite an original source or generated mirror and connect to a contract or gate.
