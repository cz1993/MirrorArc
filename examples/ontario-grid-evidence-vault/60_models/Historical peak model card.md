# Historical peak model card

This synthetic seasonal-naive demonstration estimates a future monthly peak from the same month in
the prior year plus a documented scenario adjustment. It exists only to illustrate how MirrorArc
connects model code, evaluation evidence, a gate, and an output decision.

Prohibited uses: live grid operations, alerts, reliability conclusions, trading, planning, or
stakeholder publication without independent validation and authorization.
