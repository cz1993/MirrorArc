# Electricity emissions contract

Historical electricity-sector greenhouse-gas emissions are expressed in megatonnes of CO2e.
The separate intensity source mixes historical and forecast columns and therefore requires an
explicit row-type flag before analysis. Historical observations and forecasts must never be
silently combined.
