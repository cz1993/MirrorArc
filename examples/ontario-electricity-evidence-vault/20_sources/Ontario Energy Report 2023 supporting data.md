# Ontario Energy Report 2023 supporting data

The example commits a focused subset of the 2023 supporting-data ZIP: annual demand, monthly
demand ranges, peaks, generation mix, intertie flows, and electricity-emissions series. The local
CSV files and English source notes remain authoritative.

- **Coverage:** primarily calendar 2023, with historical series where supplied.
- **Grain:** varies by file; consult the paired note file and relevant contract.
- **License:** Open Government Licence - Ontario; attribution is recorded in the provenance file.
- **Limitations:** this is an educational evidence workspace, not an operational grid feed.
