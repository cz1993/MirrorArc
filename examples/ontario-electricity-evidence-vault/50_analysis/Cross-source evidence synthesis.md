# Cross-source evidence synthesis

Demand was broadly stable year over year, while the system still experienced a pronounced
September peak. Nuclear and hydro dominated annual grid-connected generation, with gas providing a
larger summer share. Exports exceeded imports in each included quarter, and reported sector
emissions rose versus 2022.

These observations are connected, not causal. A causal or forward-looking claim would require a
separate question, licensed sources, method, evaluation, and review gate.
