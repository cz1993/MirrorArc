# 2023 peak demand finding

The included monthly source reports September as the 2023 maximum peak at 23,713 MW and a monthly
minimum of 11,402 MW, producing the widest peak-to-minimum range in the twelve 2023 rows. The
separate peak table dates the peak to September 5 at 5:00 PM EST.
